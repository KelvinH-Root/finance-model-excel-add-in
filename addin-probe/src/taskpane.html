HFG Probe

Waiting for Excel...

Tests what Office.js can do in this copy of Excel, for the HFG add-in spec. Use a blank workbook or a copy, never a live model. Everything the probe adds is named zProbe_, and Clean up removes it.

1. Automatic tests

About a minute. Excel may pause while the write and insert tests run.
